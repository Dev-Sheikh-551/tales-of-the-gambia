# Tales of The Gambia — Animation Asset Governance & Licenses

This document tracks all vector, Lottie, and motion assets utilized in Tales of The Gambia V2.

## Governance Policy

1. **Format Preference:** Bundled vector `.lottie` or `.json` stored exclusively in `public/animations/`.
2. **Offline-First:** No external CDN or remote animation URLs.
3. **Budget Limits:**
   - Micro-interaction / UI: `< 25 KB`
   - Storytelling ornament: `< 150 KB`
   - Scene celebration: `< 350 KB`
4. **Licensing:** Every asset must be free for commercial/open distribution with clear provenance.
